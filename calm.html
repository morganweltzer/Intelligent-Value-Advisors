Questions owners ask

Good questions deserve plain answers.

Don't see yours? Ask Ron directly. That's what the first conversation is for.

Ask a question
What is the 5-4-3-2-1 methodology?

It's the structure of Intelligent Value Acceleration™. We look at your business through 5 Dimensions of Intelligence, work toward 4 Strategic Outcomes, track progress with 3 Core Scores, and care for the 2 Assets you manage, your company and your life, toward 1 Goal: transforming enterprise value into life value.

Do I have to be planning to sell?

No. Many clients have no plans to sell for years. They want a more valuable company with more options. Everything that makes a business easier to sell also makes it better to own.

Is it too late if I want to transition within a year?

It's not too late. A shorter timeline changes the focus. We concentrate on the improvements that matter most to a buyer, on protecting your people, and on preparing you personally for what comes after.

Will you work with my CPA, attorney, and wealth advisor?

Yes, and that's a big part of the value. Ron coordinates your existing advisors around one plan, so you stop getting conflicting advice. You don't need to replace anyone.

What does "AI-powered" actually mean here?

We use modern analytical tools to gather benchmarks, track market changes, and spot risks and opportunities faster than manual research allows. Ron reviews it all and turns it into clear recommendations in your quarterly reports.

How does the performance fee work if value doesn't grow?

The Value Acceleration Fee™ is a percentage of the increase in your company's value, so it's tied directly to growth. The fixed Discovery fee and monthly advisory fee are agreed up front, so you'll know every cost before you begin.

Is our conversation confidential?

Completely. Your employees, customers, and competitors won't know we've spoken. Discretion is the starting point for every conversation.

What size businesses do you work with?

Founders, family-owned businesses, and private company owners in the middle market.
