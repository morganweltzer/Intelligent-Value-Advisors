Intelligent Value Advisors ("we," "us") helps business owners build, protect, and realize the value of their companies. Owners trust us with sensitive information, and we take that seriously. This policy explains what we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you request a conversation, we collect what you enter in the form: your name, email address, company, phone number (optional), the topic you choose, and any message you write.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, to deliver the site and keep it secure. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and arrange a conversation with Ron
  • To provide our advisory services if you become a client
  • To keep the website running, secure, and working well
  • To meet legal and professional obligations

Confidentiality

We treat what you share about your business and your plans as confidential. We never sell your information, and we never share it with your employees, customers, or competitors.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that help us run this website and deliver messages, such as our website host and [form service]. They may use your information only to provide those services to us.
  • Your other advisors, such as your CPA, attorney, or wealth advisor, only with your permission.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

AI-assisted tools

We use AI-assisted research tools to analyze benchmarks, market trends, and risks. We do not enter your identifying or confidential business information into AI tools that use submitted data to train their models. Ron reviews all AI-assisted analysis before it reaches you.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as we need it to respond to you and maintain our business records, [for example, up to three years], unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Client records are kept as professional and legal requirements dictate.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information you have given us, or to stop contacting you. Email ron@intelligent-value.com or call (513) 348-2900, and we will respond promptly.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, and we limit access to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is intended for business owners and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here with a new effective date.
